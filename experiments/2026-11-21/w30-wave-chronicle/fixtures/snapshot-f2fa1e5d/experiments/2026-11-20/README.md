# experiments/2026-11-20

- [mb-answerability-pairs](mb-answerability-pairs/): Overall accuracy can hide a policy that never reads the machine state: on a tiny synthetic controller fixture (3 systems, 6 rules, 12 pairs of the same command in two states that require opposite answers, 11 unpaired extras) always_permit and always_refuse score 4/7 and 3/7 raw accuracy but both 1/2 balanced accuracy and 0 correct pairs, no arm gets a pair right while the state is missing from the input, and an answerability-aware arm abstains (which fails the pair) until the needed state field is present, after which it and the rule evaluator (the same decision rule) get all 12 pairs, which is a fixture completeness check and not an independent safety test (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.24682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24682); not a reproduction)
- [w29-play-then-craft](w29-play-then-craft/): A play-then-revise loop on one tiny deterministic two-path game (3 scripted player levels, 2 integer knobs, exact Fraction metrics): a crafter that reads only per-episode report objects may change only declared knobs, inside their range, toward a target band declared before any episode; on the shipped fixture the novice's first clear moves from none in 6 episodes to episode 4 (band 3..4) on a fresh run with new players, while a noop control misses on the hard side and an overtune control misses on the easy side, and a capped or report-less episode is a HOLD that licenses no craft; the gain is fixture-authored by construction, and the paper's numbers, "engaging" and its vision player stay UNVERIFIED (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.26624](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26624); not a reproduction)
