# 실험: AI–Human Coevolution Decision Log

**날짜**: 2026-10-29

## Goal

Prototype a one-page decision log for a human+AI pair making a real choice in ≤1 day (e.g. invest memo outline or content brief).

## Hypothesis

Explicit turn-taking (human intent → AI draft → human veto reasons → joint decision) surfaces coevolution friction better than free chat.

## Method

**Mini-protocol (5 turns max)**

| Turn | Actor | Action |
|------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Human | State intent, constraints, and decision deadline in ≤5 bullets |
| 2 | AI | Draft recommendation + 2 alternatives on one page |
| 3 | Human | Veto or accept; if veto, list ≥1 concrete reason per rejected option |
| 4 | AI | Revise once using veto reasons only (no new scope) |
| 5 | Joint | Record final decision, dissent (if any), and what each party would do differently next time |

**Success criteria**

- Completed one-page log with all five turns filled
- ≥3 friction notes tagged (e.g. misread intent, over-confidence, hidden assumption, pace mismatch, accountability gap)

**Timebox**: ≤4 hours wall-clock for the pair (excluding async research).
