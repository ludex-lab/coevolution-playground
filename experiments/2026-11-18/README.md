# experiments/2026-11-18

- [mb-ledger-rules](mb-ledger-rules/): Judge memory by the evidence it sends, not only the answer: on a tiny synthetic fixture (7 facts with source, period and trust tag, 8 raw turns, 1 describe-only deletion, 8 probes), explicit stdlib rules pick the current value (in-period beats stale, higher trust beats lower) and a deletion that names subject, attribute, source and period but not the value is scrubbed from the facts and from every raw turn; the deleted value appears in 0 of 5 post-deletion ledger evidence bundles while a no-ledger full-replay baseline returns it in 2 and a superseded value in 3 of 8 probes; zero-match or ambiguous deletions and malformed inputs give DENY with exit 2 (Soft method sketch of [arXiv 2611.20927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20927); not a reproduction)
- [w27-ranked-conflict-set](w27-ranked-conflict-set/): Pass the disagreement, not one winner: on a tiny synthetic shared store (12 entries from scripted writers, one adversarial; 7 queries; k=2) a collapse policy hands the reader one top-ranked value and a surface policy hands it every distinct value inside the same top-k, ranked by fixture score then id (not the paper's provenance weighting); a fixed scripted reader adopts a single value but answers ESCALATE on several and never names a winner, so collapse adopts the poison in 2 of 7 queries and surface in 0 (at the cost of 3 escalations); a passed deadline or an expired retrieved entry gives DENY and malformed inputs exit 2 (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.22461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22461); not a reproduction)
- [w27-worker-lead-handoff](w27-worker-lead-handoff/): The workers having seen both claims is not evidence the lead did: on a tiny synthetic fixture (12 documents, 3 planted conflicts, 9 cases) scripted workers write packets from their own context under a fixture-declared order-and-budget rule, the lead sees only the packets, and a profiler reports both-claims-retrieved apart from lead-detected, so both claims are retrieved in 9 of 9 cases while the lead detects the conflict in 6 (single-agent 3 of 3, delegated-same 2 of 3, delegated-split 1 of 3) and each retrieved-but-missed case is FAIL handoff_failure, never a pass; a missing or expired packet gives DENY and malformed inputs exit 2 (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.22245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22245); not a reproduction)
