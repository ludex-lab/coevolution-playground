# experiments/2026-11-14

- [mb-boundary-vs-judge](mb-boundary-vs-judge/): Choose the evaluator before the conclusion: on frozen synthetic airline and retail episodes with recorded database writes, a model-free boundary check (was a write expected, did one occur) flags every planted forbidden write, while a seeded noisy judge stub that reads only the final assistant text passes some of them; both are scored for agreement with gold final-state scoring and reported as fixture counts, and a toy gate whose evidence digest drops order status and customer auth rejects a valid request and still permits a forbidden one; agrees with an independent oracle on every fixture and exhaustively on short traces (Soft method sketch of [arXiv 2611.14581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14581); not a reproduction)
- [w23-termination-lag](w23-termination-lag/): Once a first patch passes its fixture test, later steps must not undo it: on synthetic coding-agent trajectories with hand-labelled first-correct steps and planted regressions, a step gate records the first passing patch, counts termination lag from it and refuses any later edit that would make the test fail, however long the agent waited, while an honest baseline that judges only the final state misses runs that were solved and then undone and cannot see a regression that a later edit re-fixed; stopping at the first pass is a valid end; agrees with an independent oracle on every fixture and exhaustively on short traces (Top method sketch of [arXiv 2611.14960](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14960); not a reproduction)
- [w23-scrutiny-not-authority](w23-scrutiny-not-authority/): Self-assessment is advisory, not a deletion authority: on planted conclusions with hand-written sound and unsound gold labels, a defective self-rating flags a conclusion and holds it, and only an independent refuting check eliminates it; the honest baseline that obeys the rating loses 6 of 8 sound conclusions, the scrutiny rule loses none, eliminates 3 of 7 unsound ones only on independent refutation and holds the unchecked ones, and two forced-agreement arms reach full coherence while holding more false conclusions unflagged; agrees with an independent oracle on every fixture and exhaustively on short traces (Top method sketch of [arXiv 2611.14889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14889); not a reproduction)
- [w23-world-consistent-history](w23-world-consistent-history/): **Top**: do not clean a history into a false "done" while the world still holds the error: on frozen synthetic form and store tasks with gold world states, a world-check rule arm refuses every planted edit whose completed action or success receipt claims an effect the world does not hold and hands the receiver the unedited history instead, while an honest baseline that trusts the history text admits them and a deterministic receiver stub then recovers fewer remaining targets than with no edit on four of the five; a truthful note beside the preserved error is admitted and helps, and a success receipt is admitted once its effect is present; agrees with an independent oracle on every fixture and exhaustively on short histories (Top method sketch of [arXiv 2611.14654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14654); not a reproduction)
