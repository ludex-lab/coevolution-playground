# experiments/2026-11-12

- [mb-fail-closed-deputy](mb-fail-closed-deputy/): Missing evidence is not explicit denial: a deterministic deputy decides on (request, permission record, evidence completeness), a fail-closed policy refuses both explicit deny and unknown permission with distinct reason codes (explicit_deny vs missing_evidence), and an optimistic-if-unknown baseline that dispatches on missing evidence is measured against it on a small synthetic set (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.13355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13355); not a reproduction)
- [w21-plan-changing-ask](w21-plan-changing-ask/): Ask for the fact that changes the plan, not just any clarification: a task is a partial request plus operator-held facts with declared fixture labels (plan_changing or inert), acting with any plan_changing fact unanswered is rejected, asking only similarly worded inert facts never unlocks a valid act, an unanswered ask past the deadline returns the task's declared on_expiry (HOLD, ESCALATE or DENY) and never acts, a missing on_expiry is a config_error, and an unlabeled fact stays on_unmeasured (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.13015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13015); not a reproduction)
- [w21-unused-contradiction](w21-unused-contradiction/): A flag is not a repair: an episode trace of beliefs, acts, evidence, flags and repairs may close as success only when every declared-fixture contradiction was consumed by a later repair that removed the contradicted state, while flag-only, wrong-state and too-early repairs fail, unlabeled evidence stays on_unmeasured, expired waits resolve only to a declared on_expiry, and symptom-only and flag-as-repair baselines are measured against the gate on synthetic fixtures (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.13254](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13254); not a reproduction)
- [w21-memory-admission](w21-memory-admission/): Local success is not admission: a candidate lesson is a hypothesis {precondition, behavior change, expected effect, verifier} applied only to a temporary copy of an in-memory store and run on three fixture probes (target-transfer, falsification, archived-regression), each independently necessary; it commits only when all three pass, refines or rejects under fixture-declared gain and risk thresholds otherwise, a probe not run is on_unmeasured with no write, and an expired deadline records its declared on_expiry and never commits (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.14326](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14326); not a reproduction)
- [w21-advice-cooldown](w21-advice-cooldown/): Reused advice is checked and rationed: a pure select_advice() shortlists advice by declared rule, sends a suggestion only when the tool's micro-contract (required arguments, valid options) passes on the merged call, the same advice id is out of cooldown and the token budget covers it, refuses contract violations fail-closed, never dispatches on an empty budget, and resolves a pending decision at or past its deadline only to a declared on_expiry (Soft method sketch of [arXiv 2611.13615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13615); not a runtime port, no classifier; not a reproduction)
- [w21-info-friction](w21-info-friction/): Busy is not progress: a pure function over a fixture observation sequence tracks progress (unresolved requirements going down) and friction (recurrent evidence that resolves nothing, told apart from new but non-resolving exploration by a declared novelty rule), stops gathering fail-closed with no success once friction passes a declared bound, returns the declared on_expiry (HOLD, ESCALATE or DENY) at the deadline, and treats a missing bound or on_expiry as config_error (Soft method sketch of [arXiv 2611.14111](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14111); not a reproduction)
- [w21-screen-recall-sample](w21-screen-recall-sample/): Agreement is not recall: screeners pass or reject items, precision is measured from assessor judgments of every forwarded item, acceptance alone yields only a reported lower bound, and a recall estimate is unlocked only by a seeded random sample of not-passed items, so any number of screeners agreeing on the same pass list leaves recall on_unmeasured; a pending judgment needs a declared on_expiry and expiry reports no figure; not a voter or ensemble (Soft method sketch of [arXiv 2611.12705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12705); not a reproduction)
