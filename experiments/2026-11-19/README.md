# experiments/2026-11-19

- [mb-certified-region-delta](mb-certified-region-delta/): Leaving the operating domain is a request to recertify, not an authority cut or a shutdown: on a tiny synthetic fixture (4 state dimensions with a certified region and hard-safety limits, 2 operating-domain variables, 13 observations) a certified-region monitor classifies each observation as in-region, out-of-region, hard-safety or ODD-exit and answers with four distinct responses (CONTINUE, WITHDRAW_AUTHORIZATION, SHUTDOWN, RECERTIFY) while a naive baseline shuts down on any deviation outside the region and cuts authority on an ODD exit, so it shuts down in 8 of 13 observations against 2 and never asks for recertification (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.21811](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21811); not a reproduction)
- [w28-audit-contract](w28-audit-contract/): A pass under one numerical execution contract is not evidence that an audit verdict belongs to the model: on tiny synthetic records an audit declares its contracts (four ways of summing the same products), groups, weights text and tests before scoring, then reports a verdict per contract and the range over them (CONTRACT_DEPENDENT when the contracts disagree, never a pass from one), while scoring only some contracts, changing the weights text by one byte, or adding a test afterwards is DENY; pre-declaration is a byte comparison and a sequence number with no hash, no signing and no timestamp, and the disagreement in the fixture is authored by construction (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.23657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23657); not a reproduction)
- [w28-level-is-not-rank](w28-level-is-not-rank/): Handing a conformal level to a quantile function is not evidence the required rank was reached: with exact arithmetic the required rank for n=20 at level 0.90 is 19 (guarantee 19/21), and a linear helper lands at index 17.1 for q=0.90 or 18.05 for q=0.95 while a Weibull helper lands at 17.9, all reported WRONG_RANK under a pinned q convention, and below the feasibility floor (n=8) a helper that returns a finite end is DENY while refusing is the closed behaviour (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.23329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23329); not a reproduction)
