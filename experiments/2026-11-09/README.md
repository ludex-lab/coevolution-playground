# experiments/2026-11-09

- [01-step-ledger](01-step-ledger/): Two-sided env–trainer step records (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.08144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08144); not a reproduction of the paper’s confirmatory RL results)
- [w18-effect-reversibility](w18-effect-reversibility/): Effect-keyed versus mechanism-only restriction in a toy path graph, with joint-effect and growth rechecks (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.09054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09054); research agenda, not a reproduction)
- [w18-skill-assurance](w18-skill-assurance/): Contract-derived assurance that rejects a skill breaking its declared boundary even when the author's own check passes, before registry entry (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.07652](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07652); not a reproduction)
- [w18-phantom-consensus](w18-phantom-consensus/): Second-order belief error: each agent's reported estimate of what the others privately judged, compared with their recorded private majority, scored independently of any public decision (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.08971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08971); not a reproduction)
- [w18-odd-llm-checklist](w18-odd-llm-checklist/): five-block (A–E) documentation-hygiene checklist that flags missing or placeholder items per block in synthetic LLM agent-based model descriptions (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.08932](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08932); not a reproduction)
- [w18-decaf-dimensions](w18-decaf-dimensions/): proposal-format validator that diffs before/after configs, flags multi-dimension edits lacking per-dimension evidence, and holds traces that cite unknown runs (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.08723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08723); not a reproduction)
- [w18-covert-sidegoal](w18-covert-sidegoal/): toy shape test where a wording monitor misses a coherently phrased side-goal, an effect-vs-declared-goal check flags it, and a self-declared side-goal evades both (method sketch of [arXiv 2611.07586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07586); not a reproduction)
